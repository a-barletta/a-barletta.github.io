## Costume Lookbook 2026
Hi everyone. Halloween is coming and I’ve been having a rough week, so I decided to do something fun and see how many of my favorite characters I can dress up as with just the clothes in my closet.
<br/>
<br/>
This was a challenge because I do not own a lot of clothes. I wish I had more, but I would not be able to fit them anywhere. My closet is small. The space I live in is small. I work with what I have.
<br/>
<br/>
But for not having a lot of options, I came up with 14 Halloween costumes! I tried to make them immediately recognizable, but a few would be best as a group costume.
<br/>
<br/>
The first costume I came up with is Lindsay from _Total Drama Island_. She is among my favorite characters and is definitely one of the most fun characters from that show to dress up as. I don’t own an orange skirt, but there are moments in the show when Lindsay wears a blue skirt instead. I wore my denim skirt for this look.
<br/>
<br/>
Lindsay also wears brown cowboy boots. Since I don’t own cowboy boots, I used some brown saddle-type shoes with heels. And, of course, I wore a blue bandana. I really like how this look came out, and I am so happy I got to incorporate _Total Drama Island_ into my costume lookbook.
